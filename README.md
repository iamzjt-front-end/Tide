A lightweight pomodoro timer for macOS that lives in your menu bar.

<img width="344" height="536" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-18 at 6 17 37 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ee0cc6a8-5aa6-46c9-a853-d3e22ccbe79d" />

Weekly chart shows your completed segments. All-time total tracks your long-term focus. 

---

It's customizable. Change segment length, break durations, and how many segments trigger a long break. Adjust anytime.

The app is available for macOS 15 or later.
